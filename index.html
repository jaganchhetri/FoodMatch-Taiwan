FoodMatch Help

How can we help?

Everything you need to navigate FoodMatch Taiwan.

🔎 How does FoodMatch work?
Choose your preferences and location. FoodMatch then ranks available restaurants using information such as cuisine, distance and available dietary tags.
📍 How do I use my location?
Click Use My Location and allow location access in your browser. FoodMatch uses your location to query nearby restaurants.
🥗 How are dietary preferences matched?
FoodMatch checks available OpenStreetMap dietary tags. When that information does not exist, the app shows that the information is unavailable rather than assuming the restaurant is suitable.
💰 How does the budget filter work?
Current menu prices are not reliably provided by OpenStreetMap, so budget compatibility is shown as unavailable for live restaurants unless reliable menu pricing becomes available.
🍜 Where does restaurant data come from?
Nearby restaurant information comes from OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API. Restaurant owners and the OpenStreetMap community maintain that information.
❤️ How do I save a restaurant?
Press the heart button on a restaurant card. Favorites are saved locally in your browser and remain after refreshing this device.
🌐 How do I open a menu?
When a restaurant has an official menu link listed in available map data, FoodMatch displays an Open Menu button.
